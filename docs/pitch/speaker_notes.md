# Speaker notes

Generated by `scripts/make_deck.py` with the deck; the numbers come from `reports/`. The full talk is
about 10 minutes with a three-minute live demo; the five-minute cut is slides 1, 3, 6, 7, 8, 12.

Say the number first, then one sentence of meaning, then stop. Customers, orders and profits are
simulated; say so once at the start of the results and whenever someone asks.

## 1. TrueLift (20 s) · in the five-minute cut

Say the line and stop. Then point at the code: anyone can open the demo on a phone now; it needs no login and nothing on it can break.

## 2. Since August 2026, every restaurant-funded offer needs the owner's OTP (35 s)

The OTP turns every discount into a decision the owner signs. Today the owner signs blind: the report afterwards counts orders that used the offer, not orders it created.

## 3. ₹60 off a ₹400 order pays only if 32 of every 100 orders using it are extra (45 s) · in the five-minute cut

One order, exact. Commission is 25% of the bill plus 18% GST on it, food and packaging 37%. The break-even is 32%: below that share of extra orders, the discount loses money.

Whether commission is charged before or after the discount is a question for Eternal; we ran both.

## 4. The report counts every order that used the offer. It cannot say which ones the offer created (35 s)

This is the core of the problem. The report shows 100 orders and every one looks like a success. In our simulated market 60 of them would have happened anyway.

## 5. TrueLift adds four calls to the offer flow Zomato already has (40 s)

Four hooks. The quote is the only one the owner sees before signing; serving is a lookup, no model on the customer's path; the held-out customers are what make the weekly number true.

## 6. Try it: you own a restaurant. Create an offer (180 s) · in the five-minute cut

Live, three minutes. One person drives the laptop, the other speaks. Primary: the offline bundle on the laptop's own server; fallback: the recording. Follow the runbook in docs/pitch/runbook.md.

Say what happened, not only what was forecast: the report shows many orders, the control group says how many were extra.

## 7. For restaurants: 218 of 228 TrueLift offers pay, against 130 of 300 blanket offers (45 s) · in the five-minute cut

Lead with offers that pay: that is what an owner feels. Then the market-wide profit, which counts the orders restaurants take from each other.

- **Leader:** Restaurants make +₹4.77 lakh a week with ₹5.94 lakh less discount.
- **Domain expert:** Owners keep their discount size; TrueLift changes who sees it, the minimum order, the time and the Brand Pack reward.
- **Engineer:** Each suggestion is the best of 34 versions, calibrated on the shadow phase's random holdouts and shown only with at least a 60% chance of making money.

## 8. For Zomato: commission is higher too, and the gap widens when owners stop approving blind (45 s) · in the five-minute cut

Zomato earns commission on orders, and TrueLift has slightly fewer orders than blanket offers. It still earns more, because order values are higher and less of each bill is discount.

The chart is the risk case: owners who cannot see whether offers pay start declining them.

- **Leader:** Commission does not fall when discounts shrink, and it holds up if owners start declining.
- **Domain expert:** An owner who sees that an offer pays keeps approving it; that is the commission Zomato keeps.
- **Engineer:** The commission result has the same sign in all five seeds; the attrition scenarios are in reports/evaluation.json under consent_attrition.

## 9. The simulator knows the truth, so every estimate is checked against it (45 s)

Ranges are built to miss about one time in five: after one week they do, and later they miss less often. A pause needs the offer's own control group to show a loss, so placebo offers are almost never paused.

Say the real-data result honestly: on one dataset the simple shortcut wins.

- **Leader:** When TrueLift says keep, it is right 111 times in 111 after four weeks.
- **Domain expert:** The owner sees a range that narrows every week as the restaurant's own evidence grows.
- **Engineer:** Combined estimate: the offer's control group plus the model, weighted by noise that is re-estimated every week across running offers; the pause uses an always-valid boundary.

## 10. We audited our own work: 50 findings, all fixed. Two limits remain (35 s)

Judges trust a team that shows its own mistakes. Each fix has its guard in docs/what_we_tested.md; the corrections with before and after are in DEVLOG.md.

## 11. From prototype to pilot: measure first, change who sees offers later (35 s)

The pilot is sized to detect a rise in renewals from 60% to 70% with 80% power; the 60% is a placeholder Eternal would replace with its own figure.
