# Question sheet (English)

Print one copy per interview. The person asking reads the questions; the person writing notes the
answers in the owner's own words. About 15 minutes in all.

**Before you start:** read the consent script. Ask before any photo. Never write down a customer's
name or number.

## How you use offers (4 minutes)

1. When an offer ends, what does your offer report show you? May we see one, with customer details
   covered?
2. How do you decide whether to keep an offer or stop it? Who decides?
5. Do you use Brand Packs (a reward for regular customers)? Did they bring repeat orders, as far as
   you can tell?

## The OTP approval (2 minutes)

3. Have you approved an offer by OTP? What did that screen show you before you entered the OTP? If
   you have not seen it: have you heard of it?

## Money (3 minutes)

4. Roughly what share of your Zomato orders come from customers who have ordered before?
6. On your payout statement, is Zomato's commission charged on the bill before the discount or
   after it? May we see one statement, with customer details covered?
   Also ask: roughly how much does the food and packaging for ₹100 of sales cost you?

## Reading TrueLift (4 minutes)

Show the comprehension cards one at a time, then hand over the phone with the demo. Do not explain
first. Ask: "What does this tell you?" Mark each card: read correctly unaided, correctly after one
hint, or not understood.

## Close (1 minute)

7. If, before you approved an offer, you saw "likely +₹1,600 to +₹2,500 a week", would that change
   your decision? Would you accept 1 in 10 of your customers not seeing the offer, so that you can
   see what it really added? What would make you trust the number?

Finally: is there another owner you could introduce us to?
